Paid checkout must remain disabled until the launch terms and required legal review are confirmed. Draft-review timestamps in Supabase Auth metadata are mutable and are not an immutable consent audit log.

**Why:** The PRD requires these policies, but it does not contain approved policy text or finalized commercial/legal terms.

**How to apply:** Keep the pages visibly marked as drafts and require acknowledgment only as review of drafts, not final legal acceptance. Replace them only after user approval and move consent tracking to a durable audit mechanism before treating it as launch-ready.
